Color utilities reference the same semantic tokens as components. Theme the
token once and every matching utility, component, and layout stays aligned.

## Surface and text hierarchy

Use a surface utility on the container and the corresponding `text-on-*`
utility when the surface needs an explicit foreground role.

- Surfaces: `.bg-base`, `.bg-bedrock`, `.bg-surface-muted`,
  `.bg-surface-subtle`, `.bg-surface-default`, and `.bg-surface-overt`
- Text contrast: `.text-muted`, `.text-subtle`, `.text-default`, and
  `.text-overt`
- Surface foregrounds: `.text-on-base`, `.text-on-bedrock`,
  `.text-on-surface-muted`, `.text-on-surface-subtle`, `.text-on-surface`, and
  `.text-on-surface-overt`

## Brand and feedback roles

Brand utilities include accent, secondary, and tertiary families with muted,
subtle, default, overt, and highlight variants. Feedback utilities use
success, warning, error, and info roles.

## Common combinations

```html
<aside class="bg-info text-info rounded-md p-md">Import in progress</aside>

<span class="bg-accent text-on-accent rounded-full p-sm">New</span>
```

Use the [border utilities](/utilities/borders/) when color belongs to an edge,
and the [theme guide](/guides/theming/) when defining application-wide roles.
